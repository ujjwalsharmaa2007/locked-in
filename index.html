PDF tools

Everything runs in your browser. Your files are not uploaded anywhere.

The PDF libraries did not load. Check your connection and reload the page.

Change a line in a PDF

Open a PDF, click the line you want to change, type the new text. To add a new line anywhere, press "Add new text" and click the page. New text uses Helvetica and supports English letters, numbers and common symbols.

PDF to Word

Pulls the text out of a PDF and makes an editable .docx. Headings and paragraphs are rebuilt as well as possible. Tables, images and columns are not kept, and scanned pages (photos of paper) have no text to extract.

Images to PDF

Pick photos of notes or assignments, put them in order, get one PDF.

Text to PDF

Type or paste text, get a clean A4 PDF. English letters, numbers and common symbols only.

Merge PDFs

Add two or more PDFs, set the order, join them into one file.

Extract pages

Keep only the pages you need, for example 1-3, 5, 8-10.
